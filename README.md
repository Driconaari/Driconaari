## 🛠️ What I Do
* **LLM App Development:** Building production-ready applications powered by **OpenAI**, **Claude (Anthropic)**, and **Gemini** — from quick prototypes to full pipelines.
* **AI Agents & Automation:** Designing autonomous agents with tool-calling, memory, and multi-step reasoning. If it can be automated with an LLM, I'm probably trying it.
* **Local Model Inference:** Running and fine-tuning models locally with **Ollama** and **LM Studio** — because not everything needs to hit an API.
* **RAG & Knowledge Systems:** Building retrieval-augmented pipelines that give models real, grounded context instead of hallucinated answers.
* **Full-Stack AI Apps:** Connecting LLM backends to real frontends — auth, databases, APIs, the whole thing.
* **Cloud & Infrastructure:** Deploying and scaling AI workloads on **Azure**, **AWS**, and **Supabase**.
